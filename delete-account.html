PITBORN — Dark Arenas: Delete your account and data

Developer: Nail Muradov · App: com.nlmrd.yama

Option 1 — inside the Game (instant)

  1. Open the Game.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your player ID and all data (fighters, progress, inventory, currency, rating, match history) are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

Option 2 — by email

If you no longer have the Game installed, send an email to nl.muradov@gmail.com with the subject "PITBORN data deletion" and your Player ID (shown in Settings → Account in the Game, or in any support message you sent earlier). We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Everything linked to your player ID: game progress, inventory, in-game currency, rating and match history, technical logs. Deletion is permanent.

What is not deleted by us

Advertising data collected by the Unity Ads SDK is handled by Unity Technologies; you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. See our Privacy Policy.
