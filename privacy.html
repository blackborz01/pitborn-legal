PITBORN — Dark Arenas: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Nail Muradov ("we", "the developer") handles data in the mobile game PITBORN — Dark Arenas (package com.nlmrd.yama, "the Game").

1. Data we collect

The Game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details. You play under an anonymous player ID created automatically on your device.

  • Anonymous player ID — a random identifier generated by Unity Gaming Services when you first launch the Game. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Game progress — your fighters, stats, inventory, in-game currency, match results and rating. Stored on Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save, Cloud Code) so your progress and online matches work.
  • Device and advertising data — collected by the advertising SDKs — Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Unity Ads — when you choose to watch a rewarded video: Advertising ID, device model, OS version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction events. This is used only to show and measure ads.
  • Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase records how the Game is used: app opens and sessions, tutorial completion, fight results, level-ups and rewarded videos watched, together with an app instance ID, device model, OS and app version, language and approximate location (country or region) derived from the IP address. It does not include your name or contacts.
  • Technical logs — crash and error information needed to keep the Game working.

2. Why we collect it

  • To save your progress and run online matches.
  • To show optional rewarded ads and pay out the reward you chose.
  • To fix bugs and prevent cheating.
  • To understand how players use the Game, improve it and measure our advertising campaigns.

3. Third parties

The Game uses services of Unity Technologies: Unity Gaming Services (authentication, cloud save, cloud code) and Unity Ads. Their handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player Privacy Policy.

The Game also uses services of Google LLC: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show rewarded ads, the Google User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it, and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is used and to measure advertising campaigns (see Privacy and Security in Firebase). How Google uses data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data.

4. Advertising and consent

Ads in the Game are shown only when you tap a button to watch one. On first launch you can choose whether ads may be personalized; you can change this choice at any time in Settings. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (a Google-certified consent tool) asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown; you can reopen it at any time from Settings → Ads. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

The same choice applies to analytics: until you agree, and if you decline, Google Analytics for Firebase works without storing analytics or advertising identifiers (Google consent mode).

5. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your game data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the Game. You can also request deletion here: Data deletion request. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Analytics data is kept by Google for no longer than 14 months and then deleted automatically.

6a. Data visible to other players

In online matches, leaderboards and opponent profiles other players can see your fighter name, level, rating, equipped items and match results. Do not use your real name or personal details as a fighter name.

6b. Your rights

Depending on your location (for example under GDPR in the EU/EEA or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent to personalized advertising at any time via Settings in the Game. To exercise these rights, use the in-game deletion option or contact us at the address below. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Game data is stored on Unity's infrastructure; advertising and analytics data are processed by the providers listed above.

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.
